# The Weeknd Noir

Create a premium, cinematic, multi-page fan website dedicated to **The Weeknd (Abel Tesfaye)**.

The overall visual identity should be inspired by a dark, luxurious, cinematic nightlife aesthetic. Use the same visual direction throughout the entire website: **deep black + crimson red + dark burgundy + warm off-white**, with subtle gradients, film grain, glow, shadows, and red neon lighting.

## VISUAL STYLE

Use:

- Deep black background: #050505
- Crimson red: #D71920
- Dark red/burgundy: #4A080B
- Off-white/cream text: #F2EEE7
- Muted grey: #8C8C8C
- Very subtle red gradients and glow effects
- Thin borders and dividers
- Large elegant serif headings combined with clean modern sans-serif body text
- Cinematic photography with strong shadows and red lighting
- Subtle film grain/noise texture
- Smooth hover animations and page transitions
- Lots of negative space
- Premium, sophisticated, mysterious feeling
- Avoid anything cartoonish, overly colorful, or generic

The website should feel like a mixture of a **luxury music platform, cinematic portfolio, and official artist experience**.

## HOME PAGE

Create a dramatic full-screen hero section.

Navigation bar:

- THE WEEKND logo/text on the left
- HOME
- MUSIC
- JOURNEY
- FANS
- NEWS
- Search icon
- Dark/light mode icon

Hero section:
Use a high-quality cinematic image of The Weeknd as the main visual. Prefer official/promotional photography or appropriately licensed imagery. The image should have a dark background, red lighting, city-night atmosphere, and strong contrast.

Place large typography on the left:

"THE WEEKND"

"THE SOUND OF THE NIGHT"

Short description:
"Explore the music, the evolution, and the world behind one of the most influential artists of his generation."

Buttons:

- EXPLORE
- WATCH TRAILER

The hero should have a subtle red glow behind the subject and cinematic city lights in the background.

Below the hero, create:

### THE WORLD OF THE WEEKND

A short introduction followed by four premium image cards:

1. MUSIC
   Explore albums, singles and iconic eras.

2. JOURNEY
   Discover the evolution of The Weeknd.

3. FANS
   A space for the community.

4. NEWS
   Latest updates and releases.

Each card should use a dark cinematic image with a red overlay and have a smooth image zoom/lighting effect on hover.

## MUSIC PAGE

Create a dedicated music page.

Hero heading:
"THE MUSIC"

Show albums/eras as large cinematic cards.

Include:

- Album artwork
- Album title
- Release year
- Short description
- "EXPLORE" button

Use a dark grid layout with red hover states.

Include major eras such as:

- Trilogy
- Kiss Land
- Beauty Behind the Madness
- Starboy
- After Hours
- Dawn FM
- Hurry Up Tomorrow

Keep the design editorial and premium rather than looking like a basic music gallery.

## JOURNEY PAGE

Create a timeline showing The Weeknd's artistic evolution.

Use:

- Large years
- Cinematic photographs
- Short descriptions
- Red timeline connecting each era

Make the timeline interactive with hover/click animations.

## FANS PAGE

Create a community-focused page.

Heading:
"THE AFTER HOURS COMMUNITY"

Include:

- Fan gallery
- Favorite moments
- Fan stories
- Social-style cards
- "Share Your Story" button

Add a functional fan submission form with:

- Name
- Email
- Favorite era
- Message
- Optional image upload

After submission, show a clean confirmation message.

## NEWS PAGE

Create a cinematic editorial-style news page with cards for:

- New releases
- Announcements
- Tours
- Collaborations
- Music updates

Use large images, dates, category labels and short descriptions.

## NEWSLETTER / JOIN THE LIST

The "JOIN THE LIST" button should open a beautiful modal or dedicated section.

Form fields:

- Name
- Email

Button:
"JOIN THE AFTER HOURS LIST"

The form must actually submit data to a backend/database and display a success message after submission.

Do NOT make it a fake frontend-only form.

## RESPONSIVENESS

The entire website must work beautifully on:

- Desktop
- Laptop
- Tablet
- Mobile

On mobile, convert the navigation into a hamburger menu and maintain the cinematic visual hierarchy.

## ANIMATIONS

Use subtle, premium animations:

- Fade-in hero text
- Slow image zoom
- Red glow on hover
- Smooth page transitions
- Scroll reveal animations
- Card hover effects
- Button micro-interactions

Animations should feel cinematic and slow, not flashy.

## IMPORTANT DESIGN RULE

Do not overcrowd the website.

Use large typography, strong photography, black space, red accents and carefully positioned elements.

Every page should look like part of the same visual system.

The final result should feel like a **real premium artist website**, not a college template.

Use high-quality The Weeknd promotional/editorial imagery throughout the website, preferably official or appropriately licensed sources. Maintain consistent red/black cinematic color grading across all images.

The website must have at least these connected pages:

HOME → MUSIC → JOURNEY → FANS → NEWS

The navigation should work between all pages.

Include a working backend-connected newsletter/fan submission form.

Make the final website polished, responsive, accessible, fast-loading, and production-ready.
